Jharkhand • East Region

For eight years the elephant-rider of Saranda had carried the Vana-Kula clan’s ancestral inheritance with one rule: the sacred grove is not for sale. Vana-Raksha returned as Acharya Hari-Mohan Mahato of the Vana-Vidya Trust wearing a teacher’s khadi-kurta and an Indian Forest Service consultant’s identity-card. He learned that education without justice is a weapon, and that the saga’s nineteen bond-mechanics do not cover the moment a hero loses what he was sworn to keep through a vote he could not block.
