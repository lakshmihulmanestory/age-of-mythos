The Three-Way Faction Split

Vajra-Bhumi has answered the mind-body question one way for two thousand five hundred years: Magadhi-tradition holds the mind at Nalanda and the body at the Vajra-Sangha. The Mauryan-rise was built on both. Chapter Two splits the inheritance.

The standing Nalanda-Reconstructed-University faculty, the Magadhi-Buddhist-scholar lineages, the Atisha-Dipankara palm-leaf-tradition keepers, the standing-Magadha-archive cell’s Sanskrit-Pali-Magadhi-Prakrit translation-corps. Approximately three thousand four hundred constituents (faculty, students, librarians, archive-staff). Their argument is the kingdom-council’s succession must follow the mind-tradition; the standing-state-hero’s successor must be a Nalanda-trained scholar; the standing-warrior-clans are tools, not throne-holders. Approximately two thousand eight hundred constituents (warriors, trainers, auxiliary-police-members, family-members).
