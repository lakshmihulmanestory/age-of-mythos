What Changed Since Chapter One

In Chapter One Kurinjiselvi defeated Ravana-Vadham at the Madurai temple confrontation; the ten-stringed veena was sealed at the kingdom-council's standing-archive vault. Palinisi (Ravana-Vadham's senior administrative-disciple) was spared at the Ch 1 climax under the kingdom-council's standing-administrative-rehabilitation clause. She took the junior-clerk seat at the Madurai Sangam Hall's authorisation-clerk office at the kingdom-council's autumn 2023 administrative-reassignment session. Across the intervening eighteen months she rose to the standing authorisation-clerk seat (the office's senior administrative-control position). She rebuilt the authorisation-clerk office's standing pre-clearance-protocol into a procedural-control instrument: the standard 48-hour speech-pre-clearance window was extended through 'clarification-queries' that took weeks to resolve; the standing-handbook's standard-secondary-response was weaponised against any uninvited-speaker who approached the speaker's-mark stone; the standing rota-removal protocol's thirty-second clause's rotation-and-refreshment provision was invoked against ninety-three registered Sangam-poets across eighteen months. By the chapter's opening Monday a hundred and forty-seven poets had been silenced. The Sangam debate had paused. Acharya Bharati (Sage Agastya's eleventh-direct-apprentice; Kurinjiselvi's eighty-three-year-old mentor) was struck from the rota on the chapter's opening Monday and found dead at his Kanyakumari residence on Thursday morning (cause of death: 'natural causes'). The Acharya's leaf-letter to Kurinjiselvi predicted the kingdom-council's required response: an unauthorised speech in the council chamber to break the procedure's authorisation-rule. Kurinjiselvi walked down from the Nilgiris on Tuesday the second of February; arrived at the Madurai Meenakshi temple-city on Friday the fifteenth; entered the Sangam Hall at 10:47 a.m.; spoke the eleven syllables of the Acharya's 1989 composition's opening line at 11:02:18 a.m. before Palinisi's authorisation-clerk office had called her name on the rota-page. The Stage III Nilgiri-tahr-foreleg-first-stroke appeared across her left side at the eleventh syllable's completion. The kingdom-council's emergency-session amended the Sangam-charter across the next twelve days; the amendment was ratified 38-4 on day twelve at 3:37 p.m. The post-amendment open-assembly evening on day sixty-two ran from 7 p.m. to 4:11 a.m.: Avvaiyar-the-Younger recited the 147 silenced poems from memory across 6 hours; Tholkappiyan-the-Younger was introduced as the kingdom's standing succession-candidate; Kurinjiselvi formally stepped back to the standing-elder seat. The Madurai Manimekalai manuscript's leaf 247 showed fourteen content-original lines from day fifty-two onward; the paleographic-dating confirmed the lines as approximately 1,700 years old; the lines describe the chapter's chamber-event in standard Sangam-tradition allegorical register. The Kurinji flowers bloomed a year early at the Nilgiri Mountains' southern slope on day twelve of the chapter (the night before the charter-amendment's ratification).

The Amended Sangam-Charter — Seven Core Provisions, Ratified 38-4 on Day 12

The kingdom-council's emergency-session draft, led by standing-poetry sub-committee chair Iyer-Periya across twelve continuous-hours sessions (~14 hours per day), ratified by 38 to 4 with the four dissenting votes from senior administrative-tradition seats whose objections were procedural (they argued for abolition rather than reform):

The Seven Core Provisions • Ratified Day 12 of Chapter 2
First. Authorisation-clerk office dissolved as procedural-control institution. The 48-hour pre-clearance window is replaced by an open-registration protocol; any Sangam-poet-roll-registered poet may speak at any council session by signing the open-registration roll at the session's opening. Second. Public-objection-protocol's 24-hour-clarification-query window amended to one-hour-clarification-query window with mandatory resolution within 24 hours. Third. Rota-removal protocol amended to require: (a) 75% senior-mentor seat-vote; (b) affected poet's response-right within 7 days; (c) standing-procedural-review cell's independent assessment within 14 days. Fourth. Standing-poet-children's gallery granted observer-and-recitation rights at open-assembly evenings. Fifth. The Sangam-tradition's standing living-archive seat established. Non-rotational; held by the senior-mentor seat-vote-recognised living-archive holder. Standing-duty: attend every council session; be the kingdom's standing-recourse for unrecorded silencings, retrieved voices, and reconstructed-from-memory un-written poems. Sixth. Standing post-amendment open-assembly evening at the Sangam Hall on day 12. Open to all registered poets and the children's gallery. Living-archive seat's candidate-recognition-recitation at the opening hour. Seventh. Palinisi's authorisation-clerk seat dissolved. Standing-employment preserved through transfer to the Sangam-archives wing's junior-archivist seat under standing-mentor supervision.
— Drafted by Iyer-Periya (standing-poetry sub-committee acting-chair) with the kingdom-council's senior-mentor seat-rota (12 mentors including Avvaiyar-Periya the elder). Ratified by emergency-session 38-4 on day 12 at 3:37 p.m. Counter-signed by the Tamil Sahitya Akademi's standing-fellows and the Madurai Kamaraj University's Sangam-studies department.

Cosmic Sign • First Textual-Emergence Maw Event

The Fourteen Lines on Manimekalai Leaf 247

Tamil Nadu's Void Maw Sign • The Saga's First Manuscript-Inscription Experiment

Discovered at 6:18 a.m. on day 52 (the morning after the kingdom-council's amended Sangam-charter's day-12 ratification) by the Madurai Maraimalai Adigal Tamil-Language Public Library's vault-keeper V. Sundara-Rajan (58, 16-year vault-keeper seat-holder). The Sangam-tradition's only complete copy of the second-century-CE Buddhist epic Manimekalai by Sittalai Sattanar — a palm-leaf bundle of 2,743 leaves, sealed in a glass case at the manuscript-vault's central display-table — shows fourteen new lines of text on leaf 247 that were not on the leaf at the day-49 maintenance-check. The new lines are in the standard Tamil-Brahmi script and the standard Manimekalai Tamil-classical-Sangam akaval-meter. The ink is the standard palm-leaf iron-gall blend; mass-spectrometric analysis confirms a composition consistent with the rest of the manuscript and a paleographic dating of approximately 1,700 years. The lines are content-original — not in any of the world's extant Manimekalai partial-copies, commentaries, or scholarly-reconstructions. The lines describe the chapter's chamber-event in standard Sangam-tradition allegorical register (the rota-page's shadow, the children's tongues, the mountain's flower a year early, the unauthorised word as the assembly's charter, the manuscript that wrote itself). The lines do not change, fade, or multiply across the 8-month observation period. Catalogued at the kingdom-council's standing-cataloguing register under the amended Sangam-charter's Fifth Clause's standing living-archive provision. The Maw has demonstrated the capacity to write into a sealed historical manuscript across the 1,700-year-old ink-and-leaf threshold. Paired with Mizoram's two-headed serow as the saga's second formally-recognised cross-region Maw-sign pair: both signs are the Maw producing language no one made (Mizoram = voice from a creature that should not have spoken; TN = text on a page no one wrote).
