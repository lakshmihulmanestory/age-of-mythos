Hero Death • This Chapter
Tungabhadra dies at the climax of this story, destroying the imperial throne-stone with her own hands. The elephant-bond transfers to Krishnadevaraya-Bhrata — Bhoomi-Takshaka’s redeemed twin brother. He becomes the new Hero of Karnataka for Chapters Three and Four.

The Hero (Final Act)

Tungabhadra — The River Queen Who Broke Her Own Throne

Caught between honoring her predecessors and breaking violence-cycles. Her ward is taken legally. Her sister-villain rises with dignity rather than rage. She chooses the only act that cannot be undone — she destroys the throne-stone publicly and dies with it.

The Inheritor

Krishnadevaraya-Bhrata — The Quiet Architect

Bhoomi-Takshaka’s twin brother. Lived in his sister’s court for thirty-six years saying nothing. Defects to Tungabhadra mid-civil-war. Inherits the elephant-bond at the throne-stone’s fall. Becomes the Maha-Adhipati-in-waiting.

The Catalyst Villain

Bhoomi-Takshaka — The Restoration Architect

Returns not as attacker but as the publicly-respected leader of the Imperial Restoration. Claims the serpent-network is original Vijayanagara security infrastructure. Many believe her. Dies alongside Tungabhadra at the throne-stone.

The Three-Way Faction Split

Where Chapter One had hero vs villain, Chapter Two has three factions tearing the kingdom apart from within. None of them is entirely wrong.

Faction One
Restorationists
Rebuild the Vijayanagara Empire as it was. Bhoomi-Takshaka’s base. Bloodline-A dispossessed branch plus the old nobility. About forty percent of the kingdom.
Faction Two
Reformers
Build something new on Hampi’s ruins. Tungabhadra’s base. Sage Vidyaranya’s spirit. Krishnadevaraya-Bhrata defects to this side. Roughly forty percent.
Faction Three
Tribal Hadali / Lambani
Karnataka’s pre-imperial inhabitants, displaced when Vijayanagara rose. Tungabhadra had previously ignored them. They hold the dharmic deciding vote. About twenty percent, and finally listened to.

The Void Maw Sign

The Third Herald — a child born during the temple-earthquake — appears in Hampi at the chapter’s end. He stands in the rubble of the throne-stone, untouched and unspeaking. He is the third Herald of the Void Maw to walk Bharatavarsha. Hampi-of-the-Bronze takes his hand. Krishnadevaraya-Bhrata recognises — though he has never met one — what the child is.
