The Two Parallel Arcs

Chapter Three runs two arcs simultaneously across the same twelve months of story-time. The standing-cosmic arc and the standing-political arc converge at the standing-Sanctuary-convening climax at the standing-buried-temple at Hampi.

Arc A • Cosmic

The Beast-Rising

The standing-animals stir on their own. Standing-migration-patterns reverse. Standing-predators stop hunting. Standing-birds form sky-armies. Heroes who are standing-bonded-properly feel it first; heroes whose bonds frayed in Ch 2 are left behind. The standing-Void-Maw is no longer signs — it is sound. The standing-mouth has opened. The standing-Heralds (Karnataka Third Herald + Goa Second Herald + Arunachal First Herald) walk openly. A standing-fourth-Herald appears at the end of Ch 3, emerging from the Maw itself.

Arc B • Political

The Regional Convergence

Inside each region, the standing-surviving-kingdoms cannot stand alone. They merge — by alliance, conquest, or absorption — into a single Regional Dominant. Six dominants emerge, one per region. Each Dominant claims its regional seat at the standing-Grand-Council. The standing-restructure §1 cross-reference: all six Dominants are male. Per restructure §4 cross-reference: many female heroes step back to standing-counsellor seats supporting the male Dominants.

The Universal Phase-Structure (Per Dominant)

Each Regional Dominant story uses the standing-four-phase scaffold per standing-Chapter-Three master-plan §3 cross-reference.

Phase 1

The Animal Chooses

The standing-sacred-animal of the would-be Dominant performs a public undeniable act. Other kingdoms in the region SEE this.

Phase 2

The Bargain or the Battle

Each absorbed kingdom must decide: bargain (alliance, costing a child, a relic, an ancestor renamed) or battle (refusal, costing more). The Dominant must do both at least once.

Phase 3

The Tattoo-3 Unity Trial

The Dominant attempts tattoo-3. The standing-Unity-Trial: accept into the inner council a person hated in Ch 1 or Ch 2. Success = half-body tattoo earned.

Phase 4

The Convening

The Dominant rides to the standing-Council-Sanctuary. The standing-animal flies or walks ahead of them. The standing-Void-Maw watches from outside the sanctuary — and is heard for the first time.

The Six Regional Dominants

Thirty kingdoms narrow to six. The standing-restructure §1 male-led-succession cross-reference: all six Dominants are male. Female heroes step back to standing-counsellor seats (per restructure §4) or have died (Tungabhadra Karnataka, Sundarvani West Bengal in Ch 3, Krishnaveni AP in Ch 3 — see §4 cross-reference).

The Beasts Themselves — Cosmic Mechanics

The standing-“Rise of Beasts” is not metaphor. In Chapter Three the standing-sacred-animals act as agents. Six standing-regional beast-events frame the Sanctuary convergence per standing-master-plan §6 cross-reference.

The Climax of Chapter Three

The Sanctuary Convening at the Buried-Temple at Hampi

The standing-location: the standing-buried-temple at Hampi (Karnataka). Bhoomi-Takshaka’s standing-network of serpent-tunnels turns out to lead to a standing-chamber the kingdoms did not know existed. Krishnadevaraya-Bhrata opens it because his standing-tattoo-3 is required to open it.

Inside: a standing-stone-slab with standing-seven-seats carved into it, in a circle. Six are clean. The seventh is blackened. The standing-Six Dominants sit. Six standing-animals sit behind them. The standing-Void-Maw speaks for the first time — not in words but in the floor: the standing-seventh-seat cracks in real time, splitting from the slab. The standing-chapter ends with the Six standing up, leaving the chamber, and Krishnadevaraya-Bhrata placing his hand on the cracked seat. The seat does not crack further. The standing-cosmic-stalemate of Chapter Four has begun.
