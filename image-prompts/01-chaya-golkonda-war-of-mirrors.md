# Chaya-Golkonda — The War of Mirrors (Telangana)

**Image-generation prompt pack.** Story of Chittaranga, the diamond-cutter's son with mismatched
eyes, vs. Qutb-Chaaya, the Shadow Sultan made of living darkness, in the mirror-vaults of Golkonda.

---

```
cinematic concept art, Deccan-fantasy, Golconda fortress aesthetic, Qutb Shahi architecture,

indigo-and-silver palette, espionage thriller mood, highly detailed, intricate, volumetric light,

Indigo / midnight-blue, mirror-silver, candle gold, smoky charcoal, blood-red accents.

## 1. HEROES

```

## 2. VILLAIN

### Qutb-Chaaya — the Shadow Sultan (primary antagonist)

```
A terrifying figure that is neither man nor shadow, the Shadow Sultan, a body made of living
darkness that holds the silhouette of an old Deccan spymaster, faceless or wearing a shifting
borrowed face, flat reflective glassy eyes with no light shining from within (light bounces off
instead of glowing out), tattered indigo-black royal Qutb Shahi robes dissolving into smoke at the
edges, existing in the space between mirrors, holding a mirror-bladed sword, surrounded by faint
reflections of himself, menacing, uncanny
```
Negative: `friendly face, bright lighting, full color skin, glowing warm eyes, heroic`

**Variant — The Fifty Copies (boss fight):** 
```
fifty identical shadow-copies of the Shadow Sultan standing in a mirrored hall, each holding a
mirror-blade, every mirror reflecting them into ten thousand, the figures bleed real blood when cut
but reform from smoke, candle in the center becoming a blinding sun of reflections, overwhelming,
nightmarish symmetry
```

**Variant — Defeated:** `an old broken spy sitting in the dark, no face of his own left, weeping,
unable to remember his original appearance, four blank walls, a single candle that never goes out`
