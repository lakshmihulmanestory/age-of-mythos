# Sangam-Tamilakam — The Song That Shatters (Tamil Nadu)

**Image-generation prompt pack.** Story of Kurinjiselvi, the hermit warrior of the Nilgiris whose
truth-song counters the sonic tyranny of Ravana-Vadham, the Southern Shadow, and his ten-stringed
veena weapon.

> **Era note:** this story blends **ancient mythic** (Nilgiri mountains, Thirukkural, Kurinji bloom,
> stone chariots) with **near-future tech** (acoustic weapons, drones, jamming armor, sonar fleet).
> For tech scenes, REMOVE "modern clothing / cars / smartphones" from the negative prompt.
