# Parashurama-Kshetra — The 109th Form (Kerala)

**Image-generation prompt pack.** Story of Parashurama VII, the 58-year-old axe-bearing Kalari
master, vs. his lost brother Maya-Sura, who learned the forbidden 109th form that multiplies the
self into a thousand arms — across the backwaters and temples of Kerala.

## 7. KEY SCENES (wide cinematic shots)

**S1 — The axe at dawn:** `a scarred 58-year-old Kalari master alone in a thatch-roofed red-earth
kalari pit, swinging a massive meteorite axe one-handed so the air screams, dawn light in strips,
lonely mastery`

**S2 — The brothers, before:** `flashback, two young men training as brothers in everything but

**S3 — The grief of the elephants:** `the night the forbidden form is learned, every temple elephant

**S4 — A thousand arms:** `the most brutal duel, a lone axe-bearer cleaving through wave after wave

**S6 — The brother saved (finale):** `the axe striking the real ankle, a thousand afterimages

elephants wading into the shallows in a grey circle, Great Hornbills perched glowing at sunset, the
