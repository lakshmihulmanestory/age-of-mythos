# Age of Mythos — Motto & Album Image Brief

## I. The Motto (One Page)

### Sanskrit (Primary)

> **यतो योग्यता ततो सत्यम् । यतो ऋतम् ततो जयः ॥**
>
> *Yato Yogyatā Tato Satyam · Yato Ṛtam Tato Jayaḥ*

### English Translation

> **Where worthiness stands, truth stands.**
> **Where cosmic order stands, victory stands.**

### The Full Creed of Age of Mythos

An asteroid fell. The old order died with it.
What rose from the dust was not a kingdom — it was a question.

*Who is worthy to rule?*

Across six continents, the answer is written in blood, bone, beast, and bloodline.
Thirty kingdoms awaken on every continent.
Thirty heroes claim their thrones.
Only six are bonded to the ancient beasts.
Only one rises as Maha-Adhipati — the supreme sovereign.

This is not the story of conquest.
This is the story of the trial behind the crown.

Six volumes. Twenty-four chapters. One eternal war.
From Bharatavarsha to the Southern Cross,
from the elephant-bonded king of the south
to the navigator who reads the stars of the last sea —
the Age of Mythos is the age in which every continent
asks the same question of its greatest warrior:

*Are you worthy?*

And only the worthy survive the asking.

— *Lakshmi Hulmane*

### A. The Master Cover — "Age of Mythos" (Full Novel)

**Format:** Vertical album cover, 2:3 ratio (suitable for book cover or hero banner).

**Prompt:**

> A cinematic mythological album cover. A spherical world floats at the center of cosmic darkness, fractured into six glowing continental shards — India glowing magenta-rose, Europe sapphire-blue, East Asia crimson-red, Africa amber-gold, Americas emerald-green, Oceania turquoise-teal. A single burning asteroid streaks across the upper sky, trailing fire and gold sparks, aimed at the Indian subcontinent. Around the broken globe, six colossal silhouettes rise like guardian gods — an elephant of the south, a wolf of the north, a dragon of the east, a lion of the west, an eagle of the new world, a sea-serpent of the south — each rendered as a constellation of light against the night. Sanskrit script ("यतो योग्यता ततो सत्यम्") burns faintly along the equator in molten gold. The title "AGE OF MYTHOS" is etched at the top in tall, narrow, serif capitals — gold on black, with a soft magenta glow. Painterly, epic, museum-quality, Sabyasachi-meets-Frank-Frazetta. Deep blacks, gold leaf accents, no human faces visible. 4K, high detail, cinematic lighting, mythic grandeur.

**Color anchors:** `#0d0a14` (cosmic black) · `#c44daa` (rose-magenta) · `#d4952a` (mythic gold).

## III. Typography & Branding Notes (for whoever creates the images)

- **Title font feel:** tall, narrow serif capitals (Trajan, Cinzel, or similar), gold leaf with subtle magenta glow.
- **Sanskrit motto:** Devanagari script, used as ornamental texture rather than as a readable headline.
- **Universal palette across all six covers:** cosmic black `#0d0a14` background, gold `#d4952a` accents, plus the continent-specific accent color.
- **Avoid:** modern weapons, named celebrity faces, generic fantasy clichés (full plate armor knights, generic dragons without continental specificity).
- **Always include:** one continental beast silhouette, one fragment of script in the continent's heritage language, one cosmic element (asteroid trail, constellation, aurora, or moon) to tie all six covers into one novel.
