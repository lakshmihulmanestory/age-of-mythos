### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Sundarvani** — fishing-cat hunter; haunted by her mother's choice.
- **Kali-Tantrik** — raises the flood-dead with a skull-staff.
- **The vanished mother** — alive? undead? the central mystery.
- **Bonbibi** — forest-goddess who blessed the net.
- *Gaps:* a **father**, a **sibling/child**, a village foil.

### 3. What to Add (publishable & filmable)
- **Resolve (or deliberately deepen) the mother mystery** as the story's climax.
- Give **Kali-Tantrik a tragedy** — did he lose his own family to the flood?
- Add a **community** Sundarvani must protect (named tiger-widows).
- A **moral trap**: to defeat him she must risk releasing her mother forever.

### 4. Real-World Anchors
Sundarbans mangroves & tidal islands · Bonbibi & Dakshin Rai legends · fishing cat (state animal) · Cyclone Aila / Amphan · tiger-widow villages · climate-drowned villages.
