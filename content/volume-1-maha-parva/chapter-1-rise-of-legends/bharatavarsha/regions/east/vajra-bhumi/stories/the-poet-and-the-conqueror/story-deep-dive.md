### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Vajramukha** — gaur-fist warrior who writes poetry to cage his rage.
- **Prabhavati** — his wife, bearer of the scar; the cost of his rage.
- **Ashoka-Chaya** — the professor possessed by Ashoka's unfought wars.
- **Nalanda-Acharya** — keeper of the manuscript with the answer.
- *Gaps:* his **mother/father**, a student-poet foil.

### 3. What to Add (publishable & filmable)
- Make **Prabhavati a full character** with agency — not just the wound.
- Pay off the **manuscript** — what does "the warrior-poet" actually read?
- Give **Ashoka-Chaya genuine moral pull** (was the repentance a mistake?).
- Add a **rage relapse** with real consequences as the midpoint.

### 4. Real-World Anchors
