### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Jagannathi** — builder-queen; sees wounds, not enemies.
- **Kalinga-Yama** — the concentrated grief of the Kalinga War.
- **Her twin daughters** — one untattooed; key/lock to his power.
- **The families of Odisha** — chorus carving their own dead.
- *Gaps:* a **father/husband** figure; a sceptic who wants Kalinga-Yama destroyed.

### 3. What to Add (publishable & filmable)
- A **rival faction** that demands they *kill* the grief, not heal it — gives conflict.
- Deepen the **twin daughters** into real characters with their own arc.
- A **ticking clock**: the wheel must be finished before a festival/flood.
- Tie directly to **Ashoka's tears** — maybe a relic or descendant.
- One **action beat** where grief overwhelms a town and Jagannathi must hold the line.

### 4. Real-World Anchors
Kalinga War (261 BCE) & Ashoka's edicts · Konark Sun Temple wheels · Jagannath/Puri Rath Yatra · Indian Roller (state bird) · Odisha's grief-and-rebirth ethos.
