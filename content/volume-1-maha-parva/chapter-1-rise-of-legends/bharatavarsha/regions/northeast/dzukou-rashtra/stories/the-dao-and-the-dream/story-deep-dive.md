### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Dzulevira** — finest Naga warrior; refuses to kill.
- **Ao-Rakshas** — ghost-army builder of the old headhunting pride.
- **Her father** — wanted a son; comes to understand her path.
- **Liyaba's ghost** — the haunting old way.
- *Gaps:* a **mother**, a young warrior seduced by the old pride.

### 3. What to Add (publishable & filmable)
- Add a **young warrior** who *does* take a head — the temptation made flesh.
- Deepen the **father's arc** into a full reconciliation.
- Make the **16-tribe unity** politically fragile and hard-won.
- A **festival catastrophe** as the climax set-piece.
