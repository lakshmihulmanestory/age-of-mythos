### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Tlangvala** — cannot refuse a plea; eaten alive by his own virtue.
- **Chhinlung-Naag** — the serpent of need; voices of the dead ancestors.
- **His late wife** — died giving away her hidden rice.
- **His son** — "you are being eaten alive, Father" (Ch2 Refusing Youth leader).
- **Pu Vangchhia's spirit** — the bamboo's warning.
- *Gaps:* a daughter; a neighbour who exploits him.
