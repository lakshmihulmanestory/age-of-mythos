### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Meghanadi** — matriarch; cloud-staff; clouded-leopard bond.
- **U Thlen** — serpent reassembled from centuries of blood-bargains.
- **Her daughter** — wants to leave for Nagaland (the line's survival at stake).
- **The nameless grandmother** who planted the 500-year bridge.
- *Gaps:* a **father/uncle** figure (matrilineal context), a tempted villager.

### 3. What to Add (publishable & filmable)
- Make the **daughter's departure** the true climax (hold serpent vs hold daughter).
- Show a **villager who takes U Thlen's bargain** — greed made personal.
- Personify the **ancestral grandmothers** (a vision/chorus).
- Deepen the **matrilineal politics** as world-building.

### 4. Real-World Anchors
