### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Udayagiri** — Dawn-Walker; re-forges his spear each sunrise.
- **Tamasi** — abducted sister; queen of the cave-born.
- **The cave-dwellers** — children who've never seen the sun.
- **Baba Parashar** — the wry sage-observer.
- *Gaps:* parents; the **abductors** (who took her, and why?).

### 3. What to Add (publishable & filmable)
- Reveal **who abducted Tamasi and why** — give the wound a face.
- Give Tamasi **a lieutenant** torn between her and the dawn.
- A **breakthrough beat** — one cave-child steps into the light.
- Build the **parents'** loss into a flashback.

### 4. Real-World Anchors
First Indian sunrise (Dong/Arunachal) · Tawang Monastery · Apatani, Adi, Galo tribes · Great Hornbill & Mishmi takin/gayal · border-cave geography.
