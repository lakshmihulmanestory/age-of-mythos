### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Kanchenjunga** — monk-guardian; gentleness as the weapon.
- **Yaksha-Nidhi** — the thief who can't be satisfied.
- **His mother** — waiting 37 years; folds his letters into prayer flags.
- **The red panda** — guide and conscience.
- *Seed:* **Krodha** of Sikkim (Ch2 → becomes a Herald who walks *back into the Maw* in Ch4).

### 3. What to Add (publishable & filmable)
- Raise stakes: Yaksha-Nidhi nearly takes a **treasure that would doom the world**.
- Pay off the **mother reunion** (or its heartbreaking deferral).
- Give the thief a **partner/rival** who wants the treasures for power, not compulsion.
- Plant **Krodha** here for the Ch2–4 arc.

### 4. Real-World Anchors
Mt. Kanchenjunga (3rd highest) · Rumtek Monastery & Karmapa · Beyul (Buddhist hidden valleys) · red panda & blood pheasant · Sikkimese five-treasure lore.
