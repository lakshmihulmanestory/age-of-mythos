### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Simhavikrama** — roaring lion-bearer; earliest full-body mark.
- **Mrugendra** — passed-over brother; the Silent Army of the salt.
- **Dwarka-Nath** — the floating-temple sage who warns against pride.
- *Seeds:* **Saraswati** (the salt-queen / Maldhari line, Ch2), **Kala-Simha** (Ch2 nephew-claimant), the **Goan gaur** bond.
- *Gaps:* mother; the brothers' shared childhood (flashback).
