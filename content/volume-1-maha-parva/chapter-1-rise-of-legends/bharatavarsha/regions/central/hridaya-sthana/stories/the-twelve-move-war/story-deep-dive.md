### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Dvadashashringa** — chess-master king, twelve-pronged staff.
- **Naimisha-Naag** — serpent of perfect, despairing logic.
- **The Asian Paradise Flycatcher** — beauty that shatters doubt.
- **The Rishi of Bhimbetka** — keeper of the unanswered prophecy.
- *Seed:* **Sutrakara**, his future daughter (pays off massively in Ch2–4).

### 3. What to Add (publishable & filmable)
- **Introduce Sutrakara as a child here** — give him someone to protect, making the abstract personal.
- Externalise the mind-war into **visible stakes** (a real siege he must win the argument to stop).
- Make Naimisha-Naag's nihilism **tempt the reader**, not just the hero.
- Add a **defeat** — a move that fails, a person lost — so the win costs something.

### 4. Real-World Anchors
Bhimbetka rock shelters · Naimisharanya forest (where the Mahabharata was first recited) · Kanha barasingha · MP as India's "heart" · chess as Indian invention (chaturanga).
