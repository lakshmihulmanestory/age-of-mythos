### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Dandakarni** — foundling, wild-buffalo fury, war hammer.
- **Loh-Rakshasa** — excavator-armed demon of the industrial age.
- **Her brother** — blood-kin turned miner; believes mining will save the poor.
- **Vanvasi Mata** — forest-mother spirit who speaks through Hill Mynas.
- **Gond elders** — the tribe that raised her.
- *Gaps:* a child she protects; a sympathetic miner caught in between.
