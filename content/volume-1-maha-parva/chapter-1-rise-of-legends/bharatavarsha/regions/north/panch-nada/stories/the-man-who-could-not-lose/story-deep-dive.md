### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Panchanada** — flows between the five rivers' natures; laughs before battle.
- **Durjaya Singh** — invincible, numb, desperate to lose.
- **His youngest son** — untattooed, walks to the enemy with dal.
- *Gaps:* who **cursed** Durjaya? A foil who exploits his invincibility.

### 3. What to Add (publishable & filmable)
- Show **the origin of the curse** (a battle he should have died in).
- Pay off the **son-with-dal** as the emotional climax.
- Add a **third party** who wants Durjaya as an unkillable weapon.
- Let the **langar/community** actively be the healing force.

### 4. Real-World Anchors
The five rivers (Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) · Sikh langar & seva · Nihang gatka/chakram/kirpan · Golden Temple ethos · Northern Goshawk.
