Family Loss • This Chapter
Dzulevira survives Chapter Two and earns the Stage III mithun-foreleg mark by refusing to choose a tribe — walking the kingdom’s sacred mithun naked through all sixteen tribal valleys and answering every dialect-challenge in Hindi (the one language none of the tribes claim). Her elder sister Ka-Memcha — Moirangthem’s wife, eight-year-old Sangaputra’s mother — is killed in a Tangkhul tribal purity-rite at Ukhrul. Three kingdoms grieve the same death: Nagaland (her birth-kingdom), Manipur (her marriage-kingdom), and Meghalaya (her great-niece Ka-Sienlyngdoh’s aunt-by-marriage). The Pure-Tongue is exiled across the Burmese border to join Tamasa-Nrita. Khrieo-Mama becomes her senior advisor and the Pre-Tribal Pact is published for the first time in 600 years.

The Hero (Survives, Stage III by Refusal)

Dzulevira — The Walker Who Refused All Sixteen

Thirty-three. Tangkhul-Naga matriarch’s second daughter. Her elder sister is killed in a Tangkhul purity-rite in week three. The 89-year-old Angami elder Khrieo-Mama brings her the 600-year-old Pre-Tribal Pact in week four. She walks 27 days, sixteen valleys, naked except for ash, with one mithun. Earns Stage III at the sixteenth village (Pochury) when she answers Naga-Rakshasa’s sixteen-dialect challenge in Hindi.

The Senior Elder

Khrieo-Mama — The 89-Year-Old Who Pulled the Pact From Her Floor

Eldest Angami matriarch. Has kept the Pre-Tribal Pact (six centuries old, signed by all sixteen Naga tribes’ founders at Kohima) under the floorboards of her ancestral house for 41 years. Brings it out in week four when she sees what the Pure-Tongue is doing. Becomes Dzulevira’s senior advisor for the walk and through Volume I.

The Catalyst Villain

Naga-Rakshasa — The Pure-Tongue

Returns speaking each of the sixteen tribal dialects perfectly. Goes village to village. Convinces each tribe they are the original, pure Naga line. Distributes forged DNA-rite kits as “proof.” Confronted by Dzulevira at the Pochury village — she answers him in Hindi, the one language he cannot weaponise. Exiled across the Burmese border to join Tamasa-Nrita.

The Sixteen Tribes of Nagaland

Each tribe has its own language, its own founding story, its own warriors. The Pure-Tongue convinces each that they alone are the original Naga line. Sixteen tribes — sixteen “purest” identities — sixteen mutually exclusive claims.

Dzulevira walks the mithun through all sixteen valleys. At each she stands at the village edge naked-with-ash and reads the Pre-Tribal Pact aloud in that tribe’s dialect (Khrieo-Mama provides the translations on the morning before). The walk takes 27 days — one to three days per valley including travel.

The Void Maw Sign

The night after the Pochury confrontation, a python is found on the path Dzulevira walked. It has swallowed its own tail. It has died in the loop — the constriction has cut off its own breathing. It will not let go even in death. Khrieo-Mama reads the sign: the Pure-Tongue’s logic eating itself. The Maw signing the futility of purity. The dead python is preserved in a small museum at the Pochury village. The shrine is built around it.
