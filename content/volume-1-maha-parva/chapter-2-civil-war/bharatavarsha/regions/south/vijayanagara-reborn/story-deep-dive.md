### 2. Cast (depth & web)
- **Krishnadevaraya-Bhrata** — the future Maha-Adhipati; brother of the villain.
- **Bhoomi-Takshaka** — his twin; the villain who breaks Karnataka (dies before Ch4; named first inside the Maw).
- **Tungabhadra** — foster-mother of the orphan; dies in the temple-quake.
- **Hampi-of-the-Bronze** — the orphan child (taken by the Maw in Ch4).
- **Pulakeshi-Babu, Vidyaranya-the-Second** (hawk-tower) — the inner council.
