============================================================
  AGE OF MYTHOS  —  START HERE
============================================================

Welcome! This folder holds the whole story-world and the website
that shows it. You do NOT need any computer knowledge to read or
edit it. Here is everything you need.


------------------------------------------------------------
1) TO READ THE STORY
------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click:   index.html

It opens in your web browser. From there you can:
  • see all six Volumes,
  • click "Explore the Map" to jump to any kingdom or story,
  • click "Start Reading in Order" to read straight through,
  • use the  ‹ Previous  /  Next ›  buttons at the bottom of
    every page to move through the story in order.


------------------------------------------------------------
2) WHERE THE STORIES LIVE
------------------------------------------------------------
Everything is inside the  "content"  folder, organised the way
the story is organised:

  content/
    volume-1-maha-parva/            (Volume 1 - India)
      chapter-1-rise-of-legends/
        south/
          parashurama-kshetra/      (a kingdom)
            stories/
              the-109th-form/       (one story)
                story.md     <- the story you can edit
                images/      <- pictures for this story

Each kingdom folder and each story folder also has a small
"README.txt" telling you what is inside.


------------------------------------------------------------
3) TO EDIT A STORY  (the important part)
------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Find the story's folder (see above) and open  story.md
  2. It is just plain writing. Change the words however you like.
     - Keep the few lines at the very top (between the --- marks).
     - Start a new scene heading with two hashes:   ## THE NIGHT
     - Add a picture: put it in the "images" folder, then write
       this on its own line:        ![](images/my-picture.png)
  3. Save the file.
  4. Come back to this main folder and double-click:
        Rebuild Website.command
  5. Open index.html again - your change is there.

That's it. You never have to touch the technical files.

  (The file index.html inside a story folder is made automatically
   from story.md. Don't edit it by hand - it gets rebuilt.)


------------------------------------------------------------
4) FOLDERS YOU CAN IGNORE
------------------------------------------------------------
  assets/   - the look and the menus of the website
  tools/    - the program that rebuilds the website
  docs/     - notes for writers and artists

Questions later? Re-read this file. Happy storytelling.
